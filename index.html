How to study

Make it stick.

Use retrieval and spaced revision instead of confusing familiarity with mastery.

Evidence-informed
60-minute morning block

The deep-work protocol

10mRecall
25mLearn
20mApply
5mRecord
RecallReproduce yesterday’s learning without notes.
LearnUnderstand a small section using NCERT; use a lecture only where necessary.
ApplySolve board-style questions independently.
RecordWrite mistakes and pick tomorrow’s first question.
Spaced repetition

1–3–7–14 review ladder

Day 0 · LearnUnderstand the topic and test yourself.
Day 1 · Recall5–10 minutes without opening the book.
Day 3 · TestAttempt questions from the topic.
Day 7 · ReinforceMixed question or explain it from memory.
Day 14 · RetestCheck whether you can still retrieve it.

These are practical revision intervals, not a universal perfect formula. Shorten the gap when retrieval is weak.

Per-subject output

What counts as progress?

Accountancy2–4 complete numerical solutions, depending on difficulty.
Business StudiesOne NCERT section recalled + one application question.
EconomicsOne concept + numerical, diagram or written answer.
EnglishOne extract or one structured answer.
PE10–15 recall questions or one topic revised.

Accuracy and independent work matter more than rushing to hit a number.

Energy protection

Do not study against biology

  • Protect 8–9 hours of sleep where needed.
  • Keep gym intensity manageable during heavy fatigue.
  • Use short breaks between longer work blocks.
  • Eat and hydrate properly.
  • If unusual sleepiness persists despite adequate sleep, consider a medical check rather than labelling it laziness.
