Evidence-informed workflow

Turn a lecture into marks.

Don't just finish videos. Retrieve, practise, correct and revisit.

Study loop
01 · Watch a short blockWatch 20–25 minutes with a question in mind. Pause at natural concept boundaries.
02 · Closed-book recallHide the video and notes. Write 3–5 points, steps or formulas from memory.
03 · Solve independentlyAttempt one similar problem without copying. Then check the method and correct it.
04 · Log the errorLabel it: concept, method, calculation, format or careless error. Redo it later.
05 · Space the reviewsRevisit the chapter on Day 1, 3, 7 and 14. Recall first, notes second.
06 · Mix old + newOnce the new method is understood, mix it with earlier Accounts question types.

Research basis: retrieval practice and spaced practice have strong evidence; interleaving is useful after basic methods are understood, especially when feedback is available.
