BST · Ch. 1–6Foundational management concepts; definitions, features, importance, principles and functions. About 47 marks in the analysed sample.
BST · Ch. 9Financial Management: objectives, financial decisions, capital structure, fixed and working capital.
BST · Ch. 11Marketing Management: marketing mix, product, price, place, promotion, branding, packaging and labelling.
Economics · MacroIncome Determination (12 analysed marks) and National Income (10): AD / AS, equilibrium, MPC / MPS, multiplier, aggregates and numericals.
Economics · IEDCurrent Challenges (around 20 analysed marks): Human Capital Formation, Rural Development, Employment, Environment and Sustainable Development.
PART 2 · 28 NOV–3 DECBacklogs and remaining chapters6 days
  • Accountancy, 6–7 AM: Fundamentals / Change in PSR; Admission; Retirement / Death / Dissolution; Share Capital; Debentures; then AFS, Ratios and Cash Flow.
  • BST: Chapters 2–8, then Chapter 10 Financial Markets and Chapter 12 Consumer Protection. Chapters 7–8 and 10 / 12 must not be omitted.
  • Economics: Money and Banking; Government Budget; Balance of Payments; Economic Reforms; Comparative Development with Neighbours.
  • English + PE: keep one short evening slot for reading / writing or PE definitions, procedures and high-priority units.
PART 3 · 4–9 DECPractice, PYQs and mistakes6 days
  • Accountancy: topic-wise PYQs across partnership, shares, debentures and AFS; show complete working and formats.
  • BST: case-based MCQs, NCERT keywords, 4 / 6-mark answers, especially Chapters 1–6, 9 and 11.
  • Economics: Macro numericals and diagrams; IED application and case-based questions.
  • English / PE: one timed writing or literature task; PE recall, procedures, diagrams and application questions.
  • Classify every error: concept, formula / format, calculation, missing keyword or time pressure. Redo the question without looking.
PART 4 · 10–15 DECMock revision and review6 days
  • Accountancy: timed Partnership, Admission / Retirement, Share Capital, Debentures and AFS tests; finish with a mixed test and correction.
  • BST: sectional mock and case / long-answer correction.
  • Economics: Macro and IED sectional mocks, including numerical and diagram correction.
  • English + PE: timed sections, format / extract review and a weak-unit audit.
  • 15 December checkpoint: first pass complete, initial PYQs attempted, and a written list of weak chapters.

The BST / Economics chapter emphasis and mark estimates are from your uploaded SQP analysis. They are sample-paper observations, not guaranteed board-paper weightage. Do not skip syllabus chapters based on one sample.
